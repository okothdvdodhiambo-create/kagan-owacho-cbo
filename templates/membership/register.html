What happens next

  1. Your account is saved as not verified, so nobody can use it until you confirm the code.
  2. A 6-digit code is emailed to you. It works for 10 minutes and 5 tries.
  3. You type the code on the next page. Missed it? Use Send the code again.
  4. Your membership becomes Active with a KOC number, the account is switched on, and a confirmation email follows. You are logged in straight away.

Already have an account but never finished membership? Log in and we will take you to the member-details form.
